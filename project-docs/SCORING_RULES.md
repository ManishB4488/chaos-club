# CHAOS CLUB: SCORING RULES (APPROVED)

Status: APPROVED by the owner on 2026-11-15, who delegated the decisions ("do whatever you feel is good"). All six decisions below were approved as proposed. They can be revisited after playtesting.
All scoring is calculated on the server. Browsers only display it.

## Rules for every game

- Highest total wins. Players with equal totals share the place (two players can both be "1st").
- A player who disconnects keeps their points. Anything they did not submit in time counts as nothing for that round.
- If connected players drop below the game's minimum for 60 seconds, the game ends early and shows the scores so far.
- The end screen shows: winner, final ranking, total points, awards, and the best answer or funniest moment.
- Guests have no saved history. Leaderboards need accounts and come later.

## 1. Who's Most Likely To? (2 to 4 players)

- **Length:** 8 questions. 20 seconds to vote. A player who does not vote simply has no vote that round.
- **Voting:** secret vote for another player. Votes are revealed together.
- **Points each round:** +1 for every vote you receive. The player with the most votes (the **Round Star**) gets +1 more. If several players tie for most votes, all of them are Round Stars and each gets the bonus.
- **Tie at the end:** more Round Star wins first. If still level, they share the place.
- **Awards:** Most Voted Player (most votes in total), Main Character (most Round Stars), Biggest Drama Queen (most votes on "drama" questions), Most Suspicious (most votes on "sneaky" questions), Walking Disaster (most votes on "accident" questions). Each needs at least one vote in that category. Ties share the award.
- **Needs from content:** every question carries a category: drama, sneaky, accident, success or survival.

## 2. Impostor Word Game (3 to 4 players)

- **Length:** one round per player, so everybody is the impostor exactly once (3 or 4 rounds).
- **A round:** secret words are shown privately. Each player gives one clue of one word, 30 seconds each. Then 45 seconds of discussion, then a secret 20 second vote. A clue may not contain your own secret word.
- **Points each round:**
  - Every crew member who votes for the impostor: **+2**.
  - The impostor is **caught** only if they have strictly the most votes. If tied or not top, they **survive** and get **+3**.
  - A caught impostor gets one guess at the crew's word. A correct guess: **+2**.
- **Tie at the end:** more rounds survived as impostor first, then they share.
- **Awards:** Master of Disguise (most survivals), Sharpest Detective (most correct votes), Smooth Operator (impostor who guessed the word).
- **Update (owner requirement D-035/D-036):** the crew get the secret word. The impostor is told they are the impostor and gets NO word. Secrets are only served to the player they belong to, on that player's own request, and never in shared updates. This is tested.

## 3. Roast Your Friends (2 to 4 players)

- **Length:** 6 prompts. 45 seconds to write, 20 seconds to vote. A player who submits nothing cannot win that round.
- **3 or 4 players:** answers appear without names. Each player votes for the funniest answer that is not their own. **+2 per vote received.** The answer with strictly the most votes earns its writer **+1 bonus** (shared if tied).
- **2 players:** picking one answer would always tie, so each player rates the other player's answer from 1 to 3 stars. **Points equal the stars received.**
- **Awards:** Funniest Human (most points), Crowd Favorite (most round wins). The single most-voted answer is shown as the Best Answer, with a button to copy and share it.
- **Moderation:** submissions are screened (see Open questions).

## 4. The Fake Answer (2 to 4 players)

- **Length:** 6 questions. 45 seconds to write a fake answer, 25 seconds to choose.
- **Rules:** your own fake answer is hidden from you. A fake that matches the real answer after ignoring capitals and punctuation is refused and you write another. Identical fakes are merged and both writers are credited.
- **Points:** choosing the real answer **+2**. Each player who chooses your fake **+1** to you.
- **Tie at the end:** more correct answers first, then they share.
- **Awards:** Professional Liar (most players fooled), Truth Seeker (most correct answers).
- **Two players** works: each sees the real answer and the other person's fake.

## 5. Draw It Wrong (2 to 4 players)

- **Length:** each player draws twice, so 4, 6 or 8 rounds. The artist picks one of three secret words, then draws for 45 seconds with a random silly limit (circles only, one color, one stroke, and so on).
- **Guessing:** guesses are typed. A guess counts if it matches the word ignoring capitals, spaces and punctuation. The round ends when everyone has guessed or time runs out.
- **Points:** the first correct guesser **+3**, the second **+2**, the third **+1**. The artist gets **+1 for every player who guessed correctly**.
- **Tie at the end:** more first-correct guesses first, then they share.
- **Awards:** Sharpest Eye (most first guesses), Artist of the Chaos (artist with most correct guessers). The finished drawing replays as a Funniest Moment.

## Decisions I need from you

1. **Who's Most Likely To with 2 players.** With only two players, "you cannot vote for yourself" means both players always vote for each other, so every round ties. I propose that in a 2 player room only, you may vote for yourself ("it is me, honestly"). This breaks the original rule "cannot vote for yourself" for 2 players only. OK?
2. **Roast with 2 players** uses 1 to 3 star ratings instead of picking one answer. OK?
3. **Round counts and timers** above. They only change how long a game lasts. OK, or do you want shorter or longer games?
4. **Impostor rounds equal the number of players** so everyone is the impostor once. A 4 player game could take about 20 minutes. OK?
5. **Early end** when too few players stay connected for 60 seconds. OK?
6. **Roast and profanity.** Roasts are rude by nature. I propose blocking slurs, sexual content, threats and personal information, and allowing mild teasing. Anything blocked tells the writer to try again. Players can also report an answer. OK?

Write "approved" next to each one, or tell me what to change.
